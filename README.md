# Anna Derlemenko

Composer & Sound Designer for games, media and music technology.

## About

I'm a composer and sound designer based in Barcelona.

For more than 10 years I've been creating music, sound and audio experiences for games, media and creative projects. My work has taken me from designing entire sonic worlds for video games to developing synthesizer presets, educational content and tools for musicians and sound creators.

Alongside my professional work, I release electronic music as A.Fruit and continue to explore new approaches to sound through synthesis, experimentation and emerging technologies.
